The institute behind it

Karachi has known this name since 2006

SWK Solutions built its reputation the slow way — one distinction batch at a time, across five campuses, with a teaching staff that has largely stayed put. Cambridge Online is not a licensing deal or a spin-off brand. It is that same staff room, opened to anyone with a connection.
