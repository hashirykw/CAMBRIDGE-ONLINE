The arrangement

One faculty,
two front doors

SWK Solutions is the coaching institute — five campuses in Gulshan, Gulistan-e-Johar, North Nazimabad and Malir Cantt, running from morning to late evening.

Cambridge Online is the same institute delivered live over the internet. Not a separate teaching staff, not recycled recordings: the teacher taking your online session is the teacher standing in the Gulshan classroom.

The institute behind it

Karachi has known this name since 2006

SWK Solutions built its reputation the slow way — one distinction batch at a time, across five campuses, with a teaching staff that has largely stayed put. Cambridge Online is not a licensing deal or a spin-off brand. It is that same staff room, opened to anyone with a connection.

Nothing is diluted for the online route, because there is no separate online route to dilute.

Mixing the two

Study part on campus,
part online

A student can take Physics face to face at North Nazimabad on Tuesday and Mathematics online on Thursday, both taught by the same people who teach them in the room.

This matters most for students outside Karachi and for those sitting a retake alongside school or work — the timetable bends without the teaching changing.
